# Cross-session packet loss in v0.4.1

The optional CUDA receiver samples each eligible packet at its original submission offset using recent simulated outcomes. For three-copy traffic, the installed `TripletLossCalibrator` adjusts how much an earlier capture contributes to the next block. Eight of 21 measured windows qualify across all three seeds. Thirteen have insufficient support for at least one metric. Zero violate a supported limit.

## Adaptive calibration and release evidence

The final 55e4 session exposed a bias in borrowing a fixed amount from link 5563. In earlier October sessions, an update that lost all three copies often signaled more losses to come. In the final session, losses of individual copies accounted for more of the complete misses. Keeping the old reference at full strength produced 463–520 complete misses against 178 observed.

The estimator measures how often all three copies disappear together compared with what their individual loss rates predict. This sets a ceiling on borrowing. It then scores how well the local and reference-assisted tables reproduce the mix of all eight reception patterns in the pilots. It reduces borrowing when that mix favors local evidence. The score uses a mixture of the two tables' expected mask frequencies as a calibration heuristic. Blind suffixes are generated by the actual CUDA receiver. Each date starts fresh. Only the eight pilots in each block of 32 enter calibration.

The eight fully qualified windows were 55e4 in August and both October windows, both October windows on 5653, and September on 5565, 5599 and 55ad. August and September on 5565 and 5599 were opened after the first adaptive rule. The final revision was frozen before opening August and September on 55ad and 55dd. Of these four final confirmation windows, September on 55ad qualified and the other three had too few loss events for full qualification. In particular, 55dd September had four measured complete misses and zero simulated. On 5563, one adaptive seed fell short of the 200 simulated runs needed for p95. The fixed-reference profile on 5563 remains separately qualified.

Use these results for witnessed, ordered three-copy updates with local pilots. A receiver-only log that omits every completely lost update cannot supply the denominator for this calibration. The measurements do not establish accuracy for a new deployment without pilots or for rare outage durations below the event minimum. [Capture denominator and limits](wisun_validation.md)

On the validation host, updating one five-bit table from an eight-update pilot block took 0.59 ms median and 0.64 ms p95 on the CPU. This measured 102 updates after the eight-block history filled, over three replays of 55e4 on October 30. It excludes upload and GPU stepping. Multiple simulated worlds can reuse the same calibrated table for a shared link profile.

## Measurement and calibration

The prior comes from October 1–7 on link 5563. On the scoring link, each block contributes eight witnessed pilot updates followed by up to 24 blind updates. The estimator uses up to eight recent pilot blocks from the same day. It counts losses by replica position and by up to five previous packet outcomes. Sparse counts borrow one observation from a shorter history and one from the prior. Once a copy of the current update has landed, the second observation comes from local pilots with the same within-update history. Counter gaps and pilot boundaries break counted transitions. Each scored uninterrupted suffix starts with empty simulated history. Only the suffix lengths and pilot-derived probabilities reach the GPU. [Pinned receiver logs](https://github.com/wine-uoc/wisun_traces/tree/66c350d88d28fa39516fbc90722bdd3d9930d991)

The previous model counted every fully missed pilot triplet toward its outage estimate. Its residual packet sampler independently generated another 151–161 fully missed triplets on the original 55e4 scoring schedule. Its common probabilities across replica positions also missed the observed ordering of partial deliveries. Two of three seeds predicted a p95 run of three against four measured. The new profile models the observable packet sequence directly.

## Fixed-reference GPU baseline

All comparisons use seeds 22–24 and the existing thresholds in `qualify_outages.py`. A profile has to pass every supported gate at every seed and meet the minimum event and day counts to qualify. The two October 55e4 windows were used during development. After freezing the local within-update calibration, we scored the previously unused August, September and final 55e4 windows, and the later windows on the three additional links.

After scoring the final window, we repeated the three October comparisons using local pilots alone. The schedule, kernel, seeds, history length and thresholds were identical. On the final window this reduced predicted complete misses to 203–208 against 178 observed. All supported limits passed, with too few uncensored runs to qualify p95. In the October 8–14 window, two seeds returned to a p95 of three against four observed. October 15–28 continued to qualify. This control isolated sensitivity to borrowing the 5563 calibration and motivated the adaptive rule above.

Five interleaved rounds on an RTX 5070 Ti used eight broadcasters, eight subscriptions per receiver, three 1,000-byte offers per sender per step and 10% loss in both tables. At 1 × 1,000 agents, the history sampler took 147.66 µs median and 161.76 µs p95, versus 147.60 and 161.79 µs for the ordinary sampler. Arena storage rose from 2,479,872 to 8,527,872 bytes. At 100 × 10, both medians were 51.20 µs. These timings exclude calibration and host reads.

## Reproduce

Run inside the development container. The fidelity lane enforces the three fixed-reference regressions, all eight qualified adaptive windows, and the supported count and recurrence checks on the final 55e4 window. Any supported numerical failure in any adaptive window fails the lane. The JSON retains every seed and verdict. Use `--require-qualified` to require sufficient evidence everywhere.

```sh
PYTHONPATH=/build/network-ci/python /build/flightlib-cuda-python/bin/python tools/transport/qualify_history.py /build/network-measurements --adaptive --download --require-no-rejections --require-loss-case 55e4-final --output /tmp/adaptive-transfer.json
python3 scripts/local_ci.py network-fidelity
python3 scripts/local_ci.py network-parity
python3 scripts/local_ci.py network-performance
```
